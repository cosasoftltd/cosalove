### **The Master Blueprint: Cosasoft Dating Experience**

#### **Phase 1: Discovery & Connection**

1. **Biometric Human Verification:** Real-time video selfie matching to confirm identity during onboarding.
2. **Intent Alignment:** Mandatory goal-setting to match users with identical long-term relationship expectations.
3. **Community Commitment Contract:** Digital agreement at signup establishing the standard for respectful, intentional engagement.
4. **The Storyboard (Guided Onboarding):** High-effort, interactive profile creation requiring deep value-based reflection.
5. **The Daily Canvas:** Curated daily limit of high-compatibility profiles to eliminate infinite swiping.
6. **Vibe Constellations:** Visual representations of lifestyle, career, and family value alignment.
7. **Invisible Reputation Engine:** Internal tracking of reply rates and engagement quality to optimize visibility.
8. **Automated Behavioral Throttling:** Silent visibility reduction for users with consistent ghosting or low-engagement patterns.
9. **Shared Sparks:** Structured, mandatory icebreaker tasks required to unlock direct chat.
10. **The Safe Room:** Encrypted, time-boxed audio/video zones for initial low-stakes screening.
11. **The Meet-Up Engine:** In-app integration with local venues to schedule and book real-world dates.
12. **Verified Date Feedback:** Private, structured post-date reporting to verify real-world encounters and maintain accountability.
13. **Dynamic Profile Lifecycle:** Proactive nudges to ensure all user data and photos remain current.
14. **Empathetic AI Guardrails:** Real-time, gentle nudges to prevent toxic or low-intent language.
15. **Graduated Trust Badges:** Tiered security verification levels to indicate user reliability.

#### **Phase 2: Relationship Sustainment**

16. **The Shared Space:** A secure, private, couples-only environment isolated from the discovery feed.
17. **The Time Capsule:** A shared, encrypted archive for milestones, photos, and digital memories.
18. **Mentorship AI:** Psychology-informed relationship guidance for conflict de-escalation and communication.
19. **Objective Conflict Suite:** Logic-based tool for facilitating healthy mediation during disagreements.
20. **Date-Night Planner:** Personalized activity suggestions based on shared history and interests.
21. **Growth Milestones:** Automated, guided check-ins to navigate long-term topics like future planning.
22. **Gratitude Archive:** A dedicated space for recording and reviewing notes of appreciation.
